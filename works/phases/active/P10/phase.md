## Decisions

- **S2/S3 string split (S2):** S2 owned the identity strings bound to the mark (`BRAND_ALT_KO`,
  `COPYRIGHT_KO`, both `metadata.title`s, `OPS_MARK`); S3 the product-name prose.
- **The assistant's own name was a third spelling, 미주얼 (S3)** — invisible to the decomposition's
  `미주알` grep. **13 live prompt strings** swapped, reworded rather than romanized.
- **Binaries — `frontend/public/assets/README.md` is the authority**, rewritten by S7 for the second
  delivery. Two rules survive every supersession: a class-C derivative is **verified by pixel
  signature, never by file sha256**, and no image is ever substituted, generated or placeheld.
  *(Both re-derived independently at `P10.REVIEW` from the landed sources — signatures match, the
  near-white guard is 0, the symbol re-trims to `222x165+0+0`.)*
- **The phase takes `accept-gate --require`** — it changes what the operator sees on every page.
- **Code doc comments and dev-tooling banners stay (P10.REVIEW).** Out of scope; a sweep is its own
  job. **Deferred jobs D25 (comments + banners), D23 (P4 mail subject), D24 (`/ops` 390).** All three
  verified filed and open at the re-review.
- **R17 landed (P10.S6), `P10.S7` applied it, `P10.REVIEW` re-measured it in the product.** Record:
  `docs/reference/design/rounds/17-brand-mark-launcher/output/`. Shipped and verified: wordmark
  **nav h27 / footer h24**, ink-aligned `-7px` / `-6px` (R2's h19/h17 retired) · launcher = **32×32
  sparkle painted by CSS `mask` + `currentColor`**, R6's Saturn and both animations deleted, **0
  animations in all six states** · symbol a **first-class mark**, ink **222×165**, ink width **84%** ·
  **favicon 16/32/180** on an opaque `#0a1310` tile, served in both runtimes. **Token delta: None**
  (`tokens.css` last changed at P5). **New strings: 0.**
- **The launcher's motion exception expired; the product is not motion-free (P10.S7, confirmed).**
  R2's cosmos backdrop (`drift`/`twinkle`/`shoot`), Hero's `orbit` and the streaming caret still
  animate. Say "the launcher's exception expired". Under `prefers-reduced-motion` the whole document
  reports **zero** animations.
- **`P10.S7` corrected two arithmetic errors in the landed R17 record without editing it; both
  reproduce in the running product (P10.REVIEW).** (1) **The footer reservation is 108px, not 84.**
  `Footer.tsx` puts `.content` and `.inner` on one element and `.content` sets the `padding-inline`
  **shorthand**, so the reservation *replaces* 24px and clearance is `P − 92`; at 84px **8px of
  의견 보내기 stayed under the launcher** — the dead interaction R17 existed to fix, still dead.
  Measured at 108: clearance 16px at 768/1024/1120, and the button answers a five-point hit test and
  opens its panel at 768 · 1024 · 1120 · 1255 · 1256 · 1280 · 767 · 390, dev **and** production.
  (2) **R17's absolute nav numbers are 0.5px high** — it computes from a 52px content box; the bar is
  52px *border-box* with a 1px rule → **51px**. Measured box top **5.00**, band centre **25.60**
  against a real optical centre of **25.50**: **+0.1px**. The relationship holds; no fix.
- **Two cascade traps make correct-looking CSS inert — do not "simplify" either selector.**
  `html:root` (not `:root`) for the font tokens, because React places the linked `tokens.css` *after*
  the bundle CSS (confirmed: `tokens.css` is last in both runtimes); and `.inner:global(.content)`
  (not `.inner`) for the corner reservation. Both commented in place.
- **The mockup gate was waived by the operator** ("no mockup, just apply directly"), so the phase's
  acceptance gate is R17's only running review, and the card regroup waits there (`SIGNOFF.md` R17).
