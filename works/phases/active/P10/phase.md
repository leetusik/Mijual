## Notes for later slices

- **The two operator deliveries are class B and are landed byte-exact, never re-encoded.**
  `~/Downloads/juju2.png` (1614x1076 sRGBA, transparent, black ink) and
  `~/Downloads/favicon_and_chatbot_widget.png` (278x278, the sparkle cluster alone, trimming to
  **261x216**). Both live outside the repo and will not survive there — land them first, hash
  them, and derive everything else from the landed copies. **(from the orchestrator, for P10.F1
  and P10.F2)**
- **The white recolor has two traps and the README already documents both** — `+level-colors
  white,white` without the `-channel RGB … +channel` guard flattens alpha into an opaque
  rectangle, and without `-define png:color-type=6` ImageMagick silently writes GrayscaleAlpha.
  Re-read `frontend/public/assets/README.md` § *Exactly how the derivatives were produced* before
  running anything; verify by **pixel signature** (`identify -format '%#'`), never by file sha256
  — re-deriving changes the container, not the pixels. **(from P10.S1, for P10.F1 and P10.F2)**
- **The launcher mark is signed R6 and is being retired, but not by an executor.** `P10.F2` may
  produce and colour the sparkle asset; **it must not touch `Launcher.module.css`'s planet, band
  or two-half ring**, whose DOM order is a bug R6 already paid for (notebook note 11). The
  treatment is `P10.S6`'s to design and `P10.S7`'s to apply. **(from the orchestrator, for P10.F2,
  P10.S6 and P10.S7)**
- **`P10.F4` retires a class-A vendored export.** `public/foundations/fonts.css` is marked *"do
  not edit"*; it and the 2,057,688-byte `PretendardVariable.woff2` are superseded by operator
  instruction. Record them in the assets README the way the four `mijual-*.png` are recorded —
  what they were, sha256, and why nothing loads them. Note `layout.tsx` also hoists an **IBM Plex
  Mono `@import` to the Google Fonts CDN**; changple_web self-hosts that subset too, so the CDN
  link goes with it. **(from the orchestrator, for P10.F4)**
- **`P10.F4`'s reference implementation is `~/projects/personal/changple_web`, and it is
  in-house** — read it rather than reinventing: `src/app/fonts.ts`, `scripts/subset_noto_sans_kr.sh`,
  `scripts/gen-korean-charset.mjs`, `src/app/fonts/`, and the `noto` assertion in
  `tests/browser/smoke.spec.ts`. Two non-obvious constraints carried in its comments: the charset
  is **auto-extracted** because a hand-kept one dropped syllables and rendered them 자모 분리, and
