### 2.2 The control that makes those zeros mean something

A detector that cannot fire reports zero forever. Without touching a source file, I planted the
mismatch in the browser: a document-start script that rewrites the 404's echoed path *before* React
hydrates. Production, same build:

```

RESULT armed=false: 0/4
```

That is **D34's exact signature**, reproduced on demand on the very element F3 fixed — and note what
it also settles: the `<html>` flag does **not** suppress a text mismatch four levels below it.

I walked the product as a first-time reader (agentic, with Aside), then re-measured everything it
claimed:

1. **A chip's quote covers the chips on the line below it.** Inherent to an overlay — which is what
   fixed the line breaks — but on a five-근거 answer you must close one popover before you can tap the

2. **A Korean URL is echoed percent-encoded on the 404**: `/어디에도-없는-주소` renders as

5. **The landing countdown has no label** saying what it counts down to.

   the 404's 「sentence runs into the path with no space」 is an artifact of reading `innerText` across
   two separate block elements.
