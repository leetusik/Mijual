# Phase P11: Ask agent polish: inline citations and feature cards

_Intent: see [intent.md](intent.md)._

## Objective

Fix two operator-reported defects on the AI 질문 surface. (1) Citation chips break the prose: InlineCitation's always-mounted .quoteWrap is display:grid — a block box in <p class=prose>'s inline flow — so every chip forces a line break and a sentence resting on two or more 근거 renders as 「…입니다.[1] ⏎ [2] ⏎ [3]」. Re-cut the ask chip onto the conditionally-mounted absolute-popover anatomy components/Citation.tsx already ships (R10), so chips follow the sentence's period inline and adjacent chips sit side by side — across all three signed placements (프로즈 · 데이터 행 값 · 계산 입력), on the page and the widget, at desktop and ≤767. Server-side numbering is correct and is not touched. (2) The four hard-coded START_CHIPS_KO cards are all read-a-filing questions, exercising only search_events/get_event and leaving calculate, get_portfolio, save_feedback and get_contact invisible. Replace them with a card set — count freed from D11's 'exactly four' by operator instruction — chosen so that clicking the cards one at a time demonstrates every agent capability, keeping R16's layout and the 'card sentence is the question sent' rule. Lands before P4 so the demo video shows both.

## Decisions

_Durable cross-slice decisions. Replace a superseded line; never stack versions._

- **Q1 — the cards stay hard-coded Korean strings in `components/ask/copy.ts`; no served/derived
  variant.** `frontend.md`'s one-way rule already puts them there, and a served set costs an endpoint,
  a loading state and a fallback on the one screen that must never look empty — for nothing the reader
  sees. Aging is handled in the copy instead: P11.S2 picked companies in the corpus **now** and
  confirmed each card answers live. A data-derived set is a deferred-job candidate for P11.REVIEW.
- **Q2 — the 의견 card stays, and it files a real feedback row.** `save_feedback` fires only when the
  reader actually offers an opinion, so a card that asks *about* feedback routes to `get_contact` and
  leaves the capability invisible — which is the defect this phase exists to close. The card sentence
  must therefore read as the reader's own first-person opinion, so nothing is filed behind their
  back; the write is the existing anonymous queue row (no new endpoint, no new state, no email).
- **Six cards, one capability each** (2-column grid → three clean rows, no orphan):
  `search_events` · `get_event` · `calculate` · `get_portfolio` · `get_contact` · `save_feedback`.
  `security_check` is not a card (intent.md says why). The final Korean strings are P11.S2's job;
  this mapping is not.
- **R16 D11's two set-level rules survive the count change and bind P11.S2:** every 공시 first
  question carries a 회사 (or 접수번호) — 「이 공시」-style demonstratives only work after a prior turn
  — and the 공시 cards are **different companies × different 권리 가족 × different question shapes**.
  The three non-공시 cards (포트폴리오 · 연락처 · 의견) carry no company and do not breach it: they are
  not 공시 questions and their tools take no filing argument.
- **The six sentences, and why each routes — shipped (P11.S2).** 검색 `HLB 전환사채 공시가 몇 건이나
  있나요?` (4건, ②) · 이벤트 `접수번호 20260730000215 공시에는 무슨 내용이 있나요?` (로젠 ③) ·
  계산 `에코프로비엠 유상증자, 1,000주 보유 시 배정 신주는 몇 주인가요?` (①, 91주) · 포트폴리오 ·
  의견 · 연락처. **A bare 접수번호 is the only shape that isolates `get_event`** — a company+field
  question fires 검색 *then* 이벤트 (measured), so the two 공시-reading cards would otherwise look
  alike; D11 permits it and it is also a real capability (paste the number). All twelve presses (dev
  + production) hit the intended tool row on the **first** sentence written; **no card was rewritten**.
- **The R3 caveat in P11.S2's plan was wrong.** `get_event("20260730000215")` returns
  `appraisal_price = 2,116원` **on 로젠's current version** (quote 「매수예정가격 2,116」), not only on
  older versions — so ③ is a fully usable card family and the expired 아시아나 filing is not needed.
- **The 공시 cards deliberately avoid `SAMPLE_HOLDINGS`** (계양전기 · 대동기어 · 한화솔루션 ·
  세기상사). Seven distinct companies across six cards reads as corpus breadth; repeating 대동기어 in
  two cards would read as thinness.
- **`D11` here is R16's *design decision* id** (「D11 · 시작용 질문 카드 4장」,
  `rounds/16-smart-assistant/output/result.md`), **not** deferred job D11. The record is immutable and
  is not edited; `intent.md`'s operator instruction supersedes the 「4장」, in the P7 override style.
- **Item 1 is one slice, not two** — the three-placement / two-surface / two-viewport check verifies
  P11.S1's own change and needs its context; `P11.REVIEW` re-walks the product for the gate.
- **Chip anatomy: one component, one anatomy — shipped (P11.S1).** Conditional mount + absolute
  popover in *both* placements (R6-4). Prose `.citationWrap` = `relative; inline-block`; the row
  keeps `display: contents` and the popover anchors to `.row`, which took `position: relative`, so
  R16 §2.6's 「행 아래, 블록 전폭」 survives (measured 732px at 1280 / 354px at 390) and the
  value-column collapse is impossible. `.quoteWrap`/`.quoteClip`/`.quotePanel`/`.quoteOpen` and
  `inert` are retired. Detail: `P11.S1/result.md`.
- **The popover's ground is opaque — the phase's one deliberate deviation from R16 (P11.S1).** R16's
  `--surface-inset` was signed for an *in-flow* panel; as an overlay it lets the sentence read
  through the evidence. The 2px `--live` edge and the 180px cap stand; the fill is the product's own
  overlay ground (`#0e1a15` + 1px `--border-strong` + `--panel-glow`). No new colour.
  **The operator is told this at the gate.**
- **Close model = 칩 재탭 (R6-4) + press outside + Esc-with-focus-return (R10 §6); no `×`** (it needs a
  head row and a 28/44px control R6-4 never drew on a 10px chip). **`fit()` measures the nearest
  clipping box ∩ viewport, not the window, and flips upward** — the 620px widget thread clipped a
  popover on the last answer. `/ask` has no clipping ancestor, so there it is the plain viewport clamp.
- **Order: S1 then S2**, though independent — S1 is the demo-visible defect, so S2's live card checks
  then show correct chips. (Both are now done.)
