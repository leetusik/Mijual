- **(from P12.DECOMP, for P12.S1) — where the caret lives and the measured numbers; cite them, do not re-measure.** `AccountSlotDesktop` in `frontend/components/chrome/AccountSlot.tsx` renders `{open ? CARET_OPEN : CARET_CLOSED}` (`const CARET_CLOSED = "▾"` / `CARET_OPEN = "▴"`, lines ~87-88) inside `<span aria-hidden="true" className={styles.caret}>` (lines ~160-161); `.caret` in `AccountSlot.module.css` (lines 82-86) is `flex: none; font-size: var(--text-sm); line-height: 1`.
