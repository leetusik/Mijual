- **(from P12.S1, for P12.R1) — the screenshot instrument has two traps, and one recipe that works.** (a) `page.screenshot({clip})` takes its clip in **device** pixels against Aside's real **1440×900** window, *not* in the emulated viewport's CSS pixels, so under `Emulation.setDeviceMetricsOverride` it silently captures the wrong region. (b) `cdp.send('Page.captureScreenshot', {clip})` returned **byte-identical** images for two visibly different states — a stale capture; do not trust it for before/after work. (c) What works: full-viewport `await page.screenshot()` (PNG `Buffer` → base64) cropped outside the browser; there is no PIL on this machine, so `zlib` + a ~40-line scanline unfilter (stopping at the last row you need) is the whole tool. Two more: the CLI needs **top-level `await`** (a top-level `return` makes `await` a syntax error), and **tabs do not survive between invocations** (`listBrowserTabs()` is empty next call) while the profile's cookies do.
