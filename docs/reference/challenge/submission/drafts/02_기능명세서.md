## 1. MVP 구현 범위

**Scope statement.** Everything below is implemented, deployed and serving at
**https://jujutower.com** as of the **2026-09-02 18:00 KST** snapshot `[근거: the live service]`. The
service runs as a production build in containers on the entrant's own server, behind nginx and
Cloudflare; there is no staging URL, no demo mode and no dummy data — every disclosure fact on every
screen comes from a filing stored in the service's own corpus.

### 1.3 Deployment and data, as they run

- **Runtime.** Production build, six containers (web · API · worker · scheduler · Postgres · Redis)
  on the entrant's own server, reached through Cloudflare and a shared nginx edge. Only the web
  container is reachable from the edge; the database is on a project-internal network with no host
  port.
- **Corpus.** One store, **19 tables**, holding **1,359 events** with every filing version and a raw
  snapshot of every document, so any quoted span can be located in the document it came from
  `[근거: the production database, seeded 2026-09-02]`.
- **Refresh.** The collection pipeline runs **twice daily at 07:30 and 19:30 KST**; the notification
  send is a separate stage on its own schedule at **08:30 KST**.
- **Live board, 2026-09-02 18:00 KST snapshot** `[근거: https://jujutower.com/api/board]`:
  **감시 중 445건** (유증 12 · CB 422 · 매수청구 11) · **30일 이내 마감 40건** · **소멸 앞둔
  신주인수권 15건** · headline **2026년 소멸한 신주인수권 가치 ▷ 718.1억원** (밴드 하한 ▷ 548.7억원).
  These four numbers move with every refresh; a judge will see that day's values, not these.
- **Search and share surface, same snapshot:** `/robots.txt` **200** (1,972 bytes, ending in
  `Sitemap: https://jujutower.com/sitemap.xml`), `/sitemap.xml` **200** with **800 URLs**
  (3 static + 445 event pages + 352 issuer pages), all on the apex host.
- **Monitoring.** An external synthetic probe checks `/api/health` and the landing **every 10
  minutes** and mails the operator on failure; a **17-check read-only smoke suite** runs the whole
  chain in one command (§5.6).

### 1.4 미구현 · 향후 구현 예정 — excluded from this document by the 양식's rule

None of the following is described in §2–§5, and a judge should not look for it:

- **카카오톡 알림** — the row is visible on 알림 설정 with a bordered 「예정」 chip and **no working
  control**, deliberately: there is no server field behind it. Email is the only channel that sends.
- **마이데이터 연결 · 계좌 연동 · 거래/주문/체결** — none exists, and none is planned inside the MVP.
  Holdings are typed in by the reader; the service informs, and the reader acts in their own MTS.
- **권리 데이터 MCP 서버** — a roadmap item in 첨부1 §6, not built.
- **EB, 분할합병 · 주식교환·이전, 배당 기준일** — outside the MVP's three rights types.
- **가격 피드 / 시장 데이터 벤더** — not used anywhere; every amount is derived from the filings
  themselves.
- **운영 관제 (`/ops`)** — built, but operator-only and **심사 대상 아님**.

## 3. 사용자 이용 흐름

The order in which a stranger meets the main features after opening the 배포 URL. **The product is
mobile-first**: the 390-wide phone is a first-class path, not a reduced one — every step below was
executed at **390** and at **1280** and renders without horizontal overflow (§5.7).

### 3.1 The primary path — no account, no install

1. **랜딩 = 관제 현황판.** The reader opens `https://jujutower.com` and lands on a market-wide board,
   not a chat window and not a login wall. The first things on screen are the 2026 소멸 가치 headline
   and the 소멸주의보 strip with its ticking countdown to the nearest 청약 마감.
2. **A 종목 검색.** The hero's own field (「종목명 또는 종목코드 — 예: 계양전기」) suggests candidates
   as the reader types; choosing one opens **내 종목 조회** for that issuer.
3. **환산.** On that page the reader types their 보유 주식 수 (or taps 100 · 500 · 1,000주). The
   right's 배정 신주 and 초과청약 한도 appear with the arithmetic printed, and 「서버 전송 없음」 says
   where that number lives. If the issuer had a lapsed ① this year, **2026년 놓친 돈** states what
   was lost at that holding size.
4. **이벤트 상세.** 「상세 보기 →」 opens the filing's own page: every field with a `[근거]` that shows
   the **verbatim sentence from the original document**, the DART 원문 link beside it, and the
   정정공시 반영 line if the schedule has moved.
5. **AI 질문.** From the event page's 질문 스트립, the nav slot, or the desktop widget. The reader
   presses one of four question cards (two of which name a company the server picks from today's
   corpus) or types their own. Tool rows appear one at a time as the agent reads; the answer streams
   and ends with 근거 N건 · 접수번호 · 생성 시각.
6. **샘플 보유 종목.** Pressing 「보유 종목」 in the nav while signed out — or 「샘플 포트폴리오로
   둘러보기」 on the 로그인 page — opens, in one click, a portfolio of four **real, live** filings with
   example 보유량, so the 2층 can be understood before any account exists.
7. **계정 만들기 → 담기.** Only now is an account offered, and only because it buys something the
   anonymous path cannot have: a saved holding and a deadline mail. 계정 만들기 takes an email and a
   password (8자 이상) and there is no verification mail in the way.
8. **알림 설정.** The 시점 칩 (7일 전 · 3일 전 · 1일 전 · 당일) decide when the mail arrives; the
   default is 7일 + 1일 and deselecting all of them means no mail.
9. **마감 임박 이메일.** At 08:30 KST the reader receives one mail per matching deadline — the facts,
   the 보기 링크 back into the product, the 접수번호, and the off switch. Never a won amount.

### 3.2 Alternate entries

- **A shared link.** `https://jujutower.com/events/{rcept_no}` is a complete surface on its own, so a
  filing forwarded in a chat opens on the facts with their citations. This is the most common way a
  stranger will arrive, and it needs no account.
- **The 소멸주의보 strip.** A reader who came for the headline can go straight from the strip into
  the ranked list, and from a row into that filing.
- **AI 질문 first.** `/ask` is in the nav from every page; on a phone it *is* the surface (there is no
  widget below 768).
- **A wrong address.** Any unmatched path or a filing the service does not expose renders the
  product's own Korean not-found — 「이 주소에 해당하는 공시가 없습니다」 — with the address echoed
  back and a way to the board. It never explains *why*, because the reason is internal.

## 4. AI 및 데이터 처리 방식

### 4.1 The AI's role — a three-layer split, stated as a specification

**AI reads and speaks; determinism calculates.** The boundary is fixed in code, not asked for in a
prompt.

**Layer 1 — reading.** DART's structured fields give the skeleton only. What a shareholder actually
needs is written in prose across the 증권신고서 / 주요사항보고서 body, so a schema-driven LLM
extraction reads exactly **ten fields and no more**: ① 신주인수권증서 상장·매매기간 · 청약 취급처 ·
실권주 처리 방식 · 초과청약 조건 · 발행가액 산정방법; ② 리픽싱 세부 조건 · 콜·풋 세부 스케줄 ·
보호예수/전매제한 해제일; ③ 반대의사 통지 방법·절차; and, across all three, **정정 해석** (what
changed and how the reader's D-day moved). Every extracted value must come back **with the verbatim
sentence it came from**. Documents are never read whole: the body is cut by `<TITLE>` section and, for
very large filings, windowed around the field's anchor.

**Layer 2 — verification (deterministic, no model).** Every field carries its own gate, and the gates
judge against evidence the model never saw — the deterministically parsed label table and the stored
API rows: arithmetic consistency, date ordering, comparison with the structured field, and — on
**every** field — a **citation-span gate** that locates the model's quoted sentence character by
character in the stored document. A verdict is one of `passed` / `failed(code)` / `tbd` /
`not_evaluable(code)`, and **a check that could not run is never a pass**. **A field that fails is not
shown to the reader at all** — it is recorded with a reason code. Measured on the stored corpus:
**409 renderable field instances, 0 of them outside `passed`/`tbd`, 0 `tbd` fields leaking a value,
0 exposable events in a non-exposable state** `[근거: the stored corpus's own gate run report — a single offline
command, 0 OpenDART requests and 0 model calls, byte-identical across runs; 2026-08-20 corpus. The
project's separate exposure summary counts 418 field instances on a wider definition; the narrower
409 is the figure used here and in 첨부1]`.

**Layer 3 — speaking.** Sentences are generated only on top of verified data, and the AI 질문 agent is
an autonomous function-calling agent over **seven** server-side tools (event search, event read,
portfolio read, feedback, operator contact, an auditable calculator, and a prompt-injection guard) —
it decides which to call, in what order, over as many rounds as it needs, and when to answer. Nothing
is fetched before it speaks and no tool fires because a question matched a pattern. At the generation
boundary, mechanically: a disclosure-fact sentence reaches the reader **only** if it rests on a
verified verbatim span, every number in it came from a tool's own payload, and every quoted span is
byte-identical to the stored document. Anything else is **discarded before it is streamed** — not
flagged, not shown with a warning. **Refusal is a first-class answer** with five fixed categories
(철회 · 확정 전 · 공시에 없음 · 검증 미통과 · 계산 요청), and a refusal is citation-forced too: asked
for an amount that is 확정 전, the agent gives every known fact with its 근거 and refuses **the amount
only**.

**What the AI does not do.** All money and D-day arithmetic is **LLM-free** — 배정 신주, 단수주,
소멸 가치, the KST countdown, the 보호예수 해제일 derivation — and is unit-tested. The 철회 judgement is
deterministic (a row shape in the 정정사항 table, not a keyword). **No model training of any kind is
involved**; the 양식 asks how a 생성형 AI 모델 is *applied*, and that is the whole of this section.

**Operating envelope.** One reading model, `gemini-3.7-flash`, used in exactly two places: offline
schema extraction and the in-request agent turn. The agent runs at a low reasoning setting and logs a
**▷ cost ledger** per turn — **▷ $0.0093 per turn** on a measured sample, an estimate and never a
billed figure. Each pipeline run carries hard request/call ceilings and stops on exceeding them. The
serving path **never calls OpenDART** (it reads stored rows only) and reaches the model **only**
through the agent; both rules are enforced by code scans, not by convention.

### 4.2 Data used

**Every source is public and free. There is no price feed and no market-data vendor.**

| 데이터 | 출처 | 쓰임새 |
|---|---|---|
| 공시 정형 상세 | **DART OpenAPI** (유상증자결정 · 전환사채권 발행결정 · 회사합병 결정 등) | The skeleton fields: 배정기준일, 발행가, 배정비율, 전환가액·전환청구기간, 매수청구 접수기간 |
| 공시 **본문 원문** | DART documents (ZIP → XML): 주요사항보고서 · 증권신고서 · 증권발행실적보고서 · 정정공시 | The source for prose extraction **and the ground truth every citation span is located in** |
| 청약 **결과** | 증권발행실적보고서 | 발행 증서 / 증서 청약 / 초과청약 / 실권주 — the document the 소멸 총액 is framed on |
| 정정 이력 | the filing's `3. 정정사항` table | What changed, when, and how the reader's D-day moved |
| 사용자 데이터 | **typed in by the reader**: 보유 종목·수량, 계정 이메일, 알림 시점 칩 | Personalisation and the deadline mail. No 마이데이터, no brokerage link, no order flow |

**Stored shape.** One store, **19 tables**. The corpus chain is `corp → event → filing_version →
snapshot`, with every observed 접수번호 kept as a version and every document snapshotted with its raw
body, so a quote can always be re-located in the exact bytes it was read from. Extraction rows carry
the model, the prompt/schema version, the token counts, the ▷ cost and the gate verdicts. Excluded
filings are **retained with a suppression reason**, never deleted, so an exclusion is auditable.

### 4.4 개인정보 · 민감정보 processing

**What is stored, and nothing else.** A reader account is exactly **email + password hash** (+
timestamps) — no name, no phone, no brokerage link, no market identity, no activity trail. Beside it:
the reader's **holdings** (종목 고유번호 + 주수), the **notification chips**, and a **챙긴 돈 mark**
(filing number + timestamp, **and no amount**). Passwords are stored as a hash only; the plaintext
never persists.

- **민감정보 (금융거래정보 포함) is not collected.** No account number, no balance, no transaction
  history, no 마이데이터 connection, no brokerage link. Holdings are a number the reader typed.
- **Anonymous state never reaches the server.** The anonymous 환산 input lives in the browser session
  (「서버 전송 없음」 is printed beside it) and the sample portfolio's edits live in the browser's own
  storage. Migration into an account is **offered, never automatic**; declining sends nothing.
- **AI 질문 conversations are stored anonymously, and it is a schema property, not a policy.** The
  conversation tables carry **no account, email, IP or user-agent column and no foreign key in either
  direction**, and the session handle is randomly minted rather than derived from anything about the
  reader. The surface tells the reader this in its own words rather than claiming the conversation
  disappears.
- **Deletion is immediate and total.** 계정 삭제 removes the email at once and cascades to sessions,
  reset grants, holdings, claims and preferences.
- **The mails carry the minimum.** Only two kinds of mail exist — the deadline alert the reader
  configured and the password reset — and there is **no marketing or digest mail**. Every mail
  carries an off-switch line, and **no mail carries a won amount** (the 발행가 line states a *state*,
  never a figure).
- **Everything is first-party.** The service runs on the entrant's own server; reader data is not
  shared with, or sold to, any third party. Measured on the live pages: **no page contacts a
  third-party origin** — no analytics, no external font or script, no beacon; the only external
  references in the HTML are the DART 원문 links themselves.
- **The operator door is separate.** `/ops` has its own credential with no relation to any reader
  table, and the operator surface itself shows a **count** of a reader's holdings, never their
  contents.

**How to read this section.** Each block is a step a judge performs unaided on the 배포 URL, written

Blocks marked **[익명]** need no account at all. Blocks marked **[계정]** need one; §5.1 says how to

**One structural warning, stated up front.** This service runs on **live disclosure**, so *which*
companies appear changes from day to day. Every block below is therefore written as an **observable
state** ("an ① whose 발행가 is not yet fixed, counting down"), and every concrete company or number in
it is a **dated example from the 2026-09-02 18:00 KST snapshot**, given only so the expected shape is
unambiguous. A judge will see different names and the same states. That is the product working, not
drifting.

### 5.1 테스트 계정

### 5.4 계정 기반 블록 [계정]

These need the account from §5.1. They are stated with the same expected strings; the entrant's own
verification of them is recorded in §5.8.

**A1 · 계정 만들기 · 로그인.** `https://jujutower.com/auth/login` → 「계정 만들기」 → email +
비밀번호 (8자 이상) → the account exists immediately and the reader lands on 보유 종목. A duplicate
address answers 「이미 가입된 이메일입니다 — 로그인해 주세요.」; a short password answers
「비밀번호는 8자 이상이어야 합니다.」; wrong credentials answer 「이메일 또는 비밀번호가 일치하지
않습니다.」 — one message for both fields, on purpose.

**A2 · 담기.** From an event page press 「보유 종목에 담기 →」 (or 종목 추가 on 보유 종목), enter a
보유 주수, save. The holding appears with 「계정에 저장 · 마감 알림의 기준」 and its 진행 중인 권리
summary; 다가오는 마감 shows its countdown converted at that holding size. Following the link alone
writes nothing.

**A3 · 알림 설정.** `https://jujutower.com/portfolio/notifications` — opened **without** a session it
redirects to 로그인 (it is gated; `/portfolio` itself does **not** redirect — signed out it renders
the 샘플 보유 종목 instead); opened
with one, 「마감 임박 이메일」 shows the
수신 주소 (the account email) and the chips 「7일 전 · 3일 전 · 1일 전 · 당일」, with 7일 전 and

**A4 · 비밀번호 재설정 메일.** `/auth/login` → 「비밀번호 재설정」 → enter the address → the surface

**「[주주의관제탑] 비밀번호 재설정」**, carrying a single-use link with a stated expiry.

**A5 · 마감 임박 이메일.** With a holding whose deadline is 7 / 3 / 1 / 0 days away and the matching

**「[주주의관제탑] {종목} — {마감명} {D-표기} ({date})」**; the body carries 마감 · 기간 · 보유

with the link, then 출처: 공시 접수번호 and the off-switch line. **No won amount appears anywhere in
the mail** — the same rule as the screen's 확정발행가 전 금액 금지, kept structurally.

### 5.8 이 MVP의 한계 — stated plainly

1. **The corpus refreshes twice a day (07:30 / 19:30 KST).** A filing published in the last few hours
   may not be on the board yet. When the latest measurement is older than its threshold the board
   **says so itself**, in a banner — 「데이터가 갱신되지 않고 있습니다. 아래 값은 기준 시각의 공시
   기준이며, 그 이후의 정정공시는 반영 전일 수 있습니다.」 — above a 「기준 {날짜} {시각} KST · N시간
   전 데이터」 line. That banner is the product being honest about its own freshness, and a judge may
   well see it.
2. **An ① before its 발행가 is fixed shows no money.** The 확정발행가 is typically published about one
   business day before 청약, so the most urgent events show 배정 주수 and a 「발행가 확정 전」 chip
   instead of an amount. This is a deliberate rule (확정발행가 전 금액 금지), applied on screen **and
   in the mail**.
3. **The companies on every live surface change daily**, including the 샘플 포트폴리오's four rows and
   two of the four AI 질문 cards. That is why §5 is written by state rather than by 종목.
4. **Money figures are estimates and are marked as such.** 놓친 돈 and the 소멸 가치 headline carry the
   「추정」 tag on screen and `▷` in this document; the 증서 이론가치 is derived by inverting each
   filing's **own** 발행가 산식 — there is no price feed — and it is not a realised profit or loss.
   The screen says so: 「실제 손익은 개별 청약·매도 행동에 따라 다릅니다」.
5. **놓친 돈 is bounded by a stated 집계 범위** (유증 2026-01-01부터 · CB 2025-06-01부터). Anything
   before that window is not counted, and the page says so rather than implying zero.
6. **Three ② fields (리픽싱 · 콜·풋 · 보호예수) are read by the model** and are subject to the same
   gate as everything else; a field that fails its gate is **not shown at all**, so an occasional
   absent row is the trust rule working, not a rendering bug.
7. **The deadline mail arrives only at 08:30 KST, and only for a chip that matches.** It is not
   instant, and it is not sent for a deadline further out than the reader's chips.
8. **The reported extraction accuracy is a cross-model judgement.** 첨부1 quotes **98.6 % strict**
   (213/216, 95 % Wilson [96–100 %]) — and that figure must never be quoted without the clause that
   belongs to it: **the labels were produced by one model judging another model's extractions
   (Claude judging Gemini), not by human ground truth.** It is stated here for the same reason it is
   stated there.
9. **Withdrawn filings answer by 접수번호 but are not found by company search** — the intended
   consequence of the exposure contract.
10. **Cloudflare is in front of the origin.** A 52x page is Cloudflare reporting that it could not
    reach the origin; it is an infrastructure state, not a product screen.
11. **There are no users.** The service is built, deployed and measured, never operated. No usage or
    outcome figure is claimed here or anywhere else.
